# Safety, quality and authorization gates

Hard gates include machine guarding/interlocks, pressure and thermal hazards, energy isolation and LOTO, emergency stops, statutory inspections, product qualification, environmental permits, customer approvals, traceability, cybersecurity and human authorization.

Never advise bypassing an interlock, suppressing an alarm, skipping mandated inspection, falsifying data or changing PLC safety controls without qualified authorization. Deleting an operation is only acceptable if its protected function is unnecessary or retained by a demonstrably equivalent **approved** measure.

Every pilot needs qualified EHS/process/quality/maintenance owners, hazard review, controlled procedure, stop conditions, acceptance metrics, operator training and rollback. Agent-generated documents are proposals, not safety certification or authority to change the live line.
