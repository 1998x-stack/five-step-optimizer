# Synthetic teaching case: integrated rear underbody casting

Public concept: many stamped and joined parts may be integrated into a large cast structure. This file **does not provide Tesla factory performance data**. The repository example's cycle times, yields, costs and ROI are wholly fictional for illustrating calculations.

Question: must this structural assembly require many separate stamped parts? Delete: remove only the connections/transport made redundant by a validated redesign. Simplify: alloy, structural geometry, fill/cooling, trimming, machining, dimensional inspection and NDT. Accelerate: balance casting and downstream inspection using *final good output* rather than shot rate. Automate: qualified pouring, die monitoring, handling, traceability and defect detection.

Risks: large-part scrap, die cost and wear, concentrated downtime, capex, repairability, environmental impact and validation requirements. Never infer cost or cycle-time outcomes from part-count reduction alone. A plant-qualified engineering team must authorize any trials.
