# Evidence provenance

Trust measured timestamped plant data over unverifiable claims, while checking quality and sample bias. Prefer (1) timestamped MES/ERP/SCADA/inspection evidence, (2) approved process docs and technical drawings, (3) independent technical literature, (4) firsthand testimony, (5) explicit assumptions.

Every material claim must have source, time period, units and whether it is observed, documented, inferred or synthetic. If two sources disagree, show the conflict. A public video is not a plant measurement; a historical process description is not proof of current operational performance.

Record the input range, output formula, what costs are excluded, false-precision risk and what would falsify the conclusion. Missing data are unknown, never zero. The downloadable examples are fully synthetic teaching data.
